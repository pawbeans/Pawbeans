## On-screen facts and sources (read 2026-10-14)

| Line | Source |
|---|---|
| Vets score poop 1 to 7; 7 watery puddles; 6 piles with no defined shape; 5–4 very moist, log-shaped/piles, lose form when picked up; 3 log-shaped, holds form; 2 firm, segmented, easy to pick up (the ideal); 1 very hard, dry pellets | Purina faecal scoring chart (scale cited and redrawn, brand not shown), Ohio State University Veterinary Medical Center copy: https://vmc.vet.osu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/purina-fecal-score-chart.pdf |
| "Three is close" | Same chart; Purina's vet materials treat 2–3 as normal (UK vet centre chart: https://vetcentre.purina.co.uk/sites/default/files/materials-pdfs/VET43912%2033161%20Vet%20channel%20-%20Faecal%20score%20chart%20A4%20-%20VET43912_V2%20LO-RES.pdf) |
| Switching food too fast, a new treat, stress move the score | PDSA, "Diarrhoea in dogs" ("a sudden change in food is likely to upset the stomach and cause soft stools"; eating "something that they aren't used to"): https://www.pdsa.org.uk/pet-help-and-advice/pet-health-hub/symptoms/diarrhoea-in-dogs ; VCA Animal Hospitals, "Diarrhea in Dogs" ("Stress, especially following travel, boarding, or other changes in the environment"; "Changes in diet"): https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/diarrhea-in-dogs |
| Soft for more than a day, or any blood: call your vet | PDSA (contact your vet if ongoing for more than 24 hours; blood and/or mucus in the diarrhoea) |
| "Firmer poops, happier walks." / "support a happy gut" | BEANS brand copy (beans.sg, Digestive Probiotics); supplement phrasing "support", no cure claim |
