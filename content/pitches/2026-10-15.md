   - 1 hr: asleep on your spot on the sofa, curled. "One hour: a nap on your spot. It smells like you."

   New poses: ★ sniff, ★ run, sleep/curl/lie exist; the sigh is a face + breath FX.

4. Rate your dog's begging technique (IDEAS D31) · 25–30 s
   Hook: "Every dog has a begging move. Which is yours?"  ·  Format: levels / a "technique card" for each move with a
   dry Oswald name sticker and a 1–5 paw rating
   What it covers:
   - Cold open: a plate of food at the table edge, Miya's eyes and nose just over the rim; hook text.
   - The Stare: unblinking, total focus, a ticking clock. "The Stare. Unblinking. Patient. Unnerving."
   - The Paw: one paw taps your knee. "The Paw. A gentle tap. Then another."
   - The Chin Rest: chin on your lap, eyes up. "The Chin Rest. Full weight. Maximum eyebrows."
   - The Sigh: a big dramatic sigh, flop to the floor. "The Sigh. Dramatic. Aimed at you."
   - Saved for last, hero: The Drool Puddle, a single drop lands, a tiny splash (cel FX). "And the professional:
     the Drool Puddle." Rating stamps 5/5 with an impact.

   Ending: "Comment your dog's move." then back to the eyes over the plate (loop).
   Why it travels: funny, everyone recognises their dog, the comment is one word; light on facts.
   New poses: ★ chin rest, ★ beg stare (head up close), pawRaise and lie exist.
